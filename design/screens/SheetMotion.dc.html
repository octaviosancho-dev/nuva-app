Motion, live

Every animation answers one of three questions: where did this come from, what did I just change, or what is the app doing right now. Anything that answers none of them is decoration and does not ship. The demos below run the real durations and easings.

Reduced motion

Not “no motion”. With useReducedMotion() true, transforms and loops stop, opacity cross-fades at 160ms remain, and every state change still happens. Pose swaps still fire, because the pose carries meaning.

  • Screen transition → cross-fade, no slide
  • Validation → card fades, number appears at its value
  • Trail → segment fills instantly, the moth jumps
  • Vera → pose swaps, no breathe, no motes
  • Chart → line and area render complete
